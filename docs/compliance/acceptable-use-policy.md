# Acceptable Use Policy

## Overview

This policy defines acceptable use of the Falcon platform and its supporting services.

Its purpose is to protect platform integrity, operational reliability, and user safety.

---

# Acceptable Activities

Authorized users may:

• Operate supported aircraft

• View telemetry

• Execute authorized commands

• Access documentation

• Perform testing

• Develop approved integrations

---

# Prohibited Activities

Users must not:

• Attempt unauthorized access

• Bypass authentication

• Interfere with aircraft operations

• Flood platform services

• Perform denial-of-service attacks

• Introduce malware

• Tamper with telemetry

• Spoof aircraft identities

• Intercept communications

---

# Security

Users are expected to:

• Protect credentials

• Report suspected vulnerabilities

• Use secure networks

• Keep software updated

---

# Operational Safety

Operators are responsible for ensuring that:

• Aircraft are operated safely

• Local regulations are followed

• Commands are verified before execution

• Emergency procedures are understood

Falcon assists operators but does not replace responsible decision-making.

---

# Monitoring

Operational activity may be logged for:

• Security

• Diagnostics

• Reliability

• Incident investigation

---

# Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in:

• Account suspension

• Removal of access

• Investigation

• Legal action where appropriate

---

# Summary

Responsible platform usage protects both the Falcon ecosystem and the safety of connected aircraft operations.
