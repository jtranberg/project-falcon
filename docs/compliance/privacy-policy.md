# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

---

# Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Falcon collects, uses, stores, and protects operational information generated while using the Falcon platform.

Falcon is currently private software intended for authorized operators and internal development.

---

# Information We Collect

Falcon may collect operational information including:

• Aircraft identifiers

• Telemetry

• Flight commands

• Operational logs

• System health information

• Connection timestamps

• Application version information

The platform does not intentionally collect unnecessary personal information.

---

# Telemetry

Telemetry may include:

• Aircraft position

• Altitude

• Heading

• Speed

• Flight mode

• Battery information

• GPS status

• Signal strength

Telemetry is used exclusively for aircraft operation, monitoring, diagnostics, and platform development.

---

# Operational Logs

Falcon records operational events including:

• Gateway connections

• Aircraft status

• Flight commands

• Telemetry events

• System errors

• Incident information

These logs assist with troubleshooting, reliability improvements, and operational monitoring.

---

# Cookies

The Drone Console currently does not rely on advertising or tracking cookies.

Future authenticated services may use essential session cookies.

---

# Information Sharing

Falcon does not sell operational data.

Information is only shared between platform services required to support aircraft operations.

---

# Data Security

Falcon protects operational information using:

• TLS encryption

• Secure cloud infrastructure

• Environment-managed credentials

• Access controls

---

# Data Retention

Operational data may be retained for:

• Diagnostics

• Incident investigation

• Platform improvement

Retention periods may change as Falcon evolves.

---

# Your Rights

Authorized users may request:

• Information about stored operational data

• Correction of inaccurate information

• Deletion of data where applicable

---

# Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Falcon evolves.

Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date.

---

# Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy should be directed to the Falcon development team.
