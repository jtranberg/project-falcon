# Responsible Disclosure Policy

## Overview

The Falcon project encourages responsible reporting of security vulnerabilities.

Security researchers, users, and contributors who discover potential vulnerabilities are encouraged to report them privately to allow time for investigation and remediation.

---

# Reporting a Vulnerability

When reporting a potential security issue, include:

• A clear description of the issue

• Steps to reproduce

• Affected component

• Software version

• Supporting logs or screenshots, if available

• Potential impact

Reports should provide enough information for the issue to be reproduced and validated.

---

# Preferred Reporting Method

Security issues should be reported through a private communication channel designated by the Falcon development team.

Please do not disclose vulnerabilities publicly until they have been investigated and resolved.

---

# Our Commitment

The Falcon development team will make reasonable efforts to:

• Acknowledge receipt of the report

• Investigate the issue

• Assess potential impact

• Develop and deploy an appropriate fix

• Communicate progress when practical

---

# Coordinated Disclosure

Public disclosure is encouraged only after:

• The issue has been resolved, or

• A mutually agreed disclosure timeline has been reached.

This approach helps protect users while allowing sufficient time for remediation.

---

# Good Faith Research

Security research performed in good faith and reported responsibly is welcomed.

Researchers acting responsibly are expected to:

• Avoid disrupting platform availability

• Protect user privacy

• Avoid unauthorized access to data

• Refrain from exploiting vulnerabilities beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the issue

---

# Out of Scope

The following are generally considered out of scope:

• Denial-of-service testing against production systems

• Social engineering attacks

• Physical security attacks

• Vulnerabilities in third-party services outside Falcon's control

---

# Recognition

The Falcon project values responsible security research and appreciates reports that contribute to improving the platform's security and reliability.

---

# Policy Updates

This Responsible Disclosure Policy may be updated as the Falcon platform evolves.

---

# Summary

Responsible disclosure strengthens the Falcon platform by fostering collaboration with the security community while protecting users through coordinated vulnerability reporting and remediation.
