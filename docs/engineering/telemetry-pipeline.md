# Telemetry Pipeline

## Overview

Telemetry is continuously generated by the aircraft and streamed through the Falcon platform.

The pipeline is optimized for low latency while remaining resilient to temporary connectivity issues.

---

# Pipeline

Aircraft

↓

Telemetry Generation

↓

MQTT Publish

↓

HiveMQ

↓

Gateway

↓

Socket.IO

↓

Drone Console

↓

Operator Display

---
